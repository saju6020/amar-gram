Who we are

Amar Gram is an online and counter shop bringing organic, village-sourced goods from across Bangladesh to Dhaka.

  • Rice, lentils and mustard oil from growers' co-operatives
  • Sundarbans honey, khejur gur and pure ghee
  • Everyday groceries and household goods

Frequently asked questions

Can I return an opened jar of honey?

Yes, within 7 days if something is wrong with it — bring it to any outlet or ask the rider to collect it with your next delivery.

Do you offer same-day delivery?

Inside Dhaka, orders placed before 12:00 can usually be delivered the same evening; choose a slot at checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, card in person, and bank transfer. Online orders can be paid on delivery.
